Grid-forming ESS for AI data-center loads

Developed analytical and EMT models of a synchronous-generator-based power system supported by a 2 MW grid-forming supercapacitor ESS during fast, repetitive multi-megawatt load transitions.

  • Compared grid-only, droop, conventional VSG, and load-aware VSG control.
  • Evaluated virtual inertia, damping, grid strength, power sharing, and measurement delay.
  • Measured RoCoF, frequency nadir, grid power, converter power, and energy use.
